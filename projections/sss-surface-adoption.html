The seam

SSS names the layer the design previously blurred: between what the domain has and how a page looks sits the decision of what matters here. One inequality carries the whole protocol:

CANON ≠ SURFACE ≠ EXPRESSION ≠ REPRESENTATION — the domain owns what exists; the surface owns contextual participation; expression owns permitted appearance; the representation is what a participant actually encounters.
